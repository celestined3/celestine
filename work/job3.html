PASS Leader - Volunteering

Overview

In my second year, I became a PASS leader for younger students doing my course. This involved planning socials, guiding peers struggling with adjusting to university life through weekly advice meetings or activities and creating presentations to explain unit concepts, all of which developed my communication and organisation skills. I wanted to volunteer for this scheme and applied because I am committed to helping make student life the best it can be – I want to make sure everyone has the opportunity to enjoy their time at UoM as much as I have so far.
