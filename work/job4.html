Sales Assistant - The Original Factory Shop

Overview

My main job was working behind the tills because I have good people skills and am able to multi-task. I am someone who thrives under pressure and was able to learn and manage issues, returns, phone calls and the tills at the same time. I joined as an employee during a very busy period, meaning I had to learn the job quickly and constantly adapt to new situations. Despite a rather involved first month, I excelled in my probation period and was promoted to higher responsibilities.

One of those responsibilities was managing the balloon stand. This meant organizing orders, learning how to use the helium tank, and swiftly making the customer’s requested designs. This role enabled me to improve my communication skills, as I needed to make sure the customer did not get bored, while simultaneously making balloon arches.

When the shop was less busy, I would unpack orders and arrange shelves with my colleagues. This not only taught me about organisation, but it also highlighted my enjoyment of working as a team with other people. Dividing tasks amongst ourselves allowed us to complete the work in the most efficient way together.

As an engineer, I always look for ways to improve and develop existing infrastructure. I came up with a way of organizing shifts, which my manager decided to implement. Keeping specific records of availability and future shifts meant that a smoother operation of shift timing and job training occurred.
