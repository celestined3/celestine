Sales Assistant - The Original Factory Shop

Overview

My main role was working behind the tills, which meant getting used to handling lots of different situations throughout the day. I joined during a particularly busy seasonal period, so there was a lot to learn in a short amount of time. Alongside serving customers, I dealt with returns, phone calls and tills, often having to switch between different tasks as things came up. It was a good introduction to working in a fast paced environment and becoming more comfortable dealing with people I had never met before.

I also took on responsibility for the balloon stand, which was something completely new to me. I learned how to use the helium tank, organise orders and make the different designs customers requested. It was quite a different kind of customer interaction, as I would often be making balloon arches while keeping the customer engaged and making sure their order was what they wanted.

When the shop was quieter, I worked with my colleagues unpacking deliveries and organising the shelves. I enjoyed the teamwork involved in getting through a large delivery together, particularly when we could divide up the work and help each other where needed.

I also noticed that keeping track of shift availability and upcoming training could be made simpler, so I put together a way of organising this information which my manager decided to implement. It was a small change but it made coordinating shifts and training easier for the team.
