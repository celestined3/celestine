Shadowed an Engineer - Harris RCS

Overview

I arranged this one day work experience at Harris RCS myself after contacting the company and asking if I could come in and shadow an engineer. I was still at school at the time, so it was one of my first opportunities to see what engineering looked like outside of the classroom. I spent the day being shown around the workshop and warehouse, seeing how different parts of the business worked and getting a feel for the equipment and processes involved.

One of the most memorable parts was getting to use their new robotic arm and program it using an app on an iPad, where I could build up its movements by dragging and dropping commands. I also saw how components were measured to extremely precise tolerances using specialist inspection equipment and had the chance to see a small open frame 3D printer in operation. It was a really useful introduction to the practical side of engineering and gave me an early look at the combination of manufacturing, automation and hands on problem solving that goes into making engineering systems work.
