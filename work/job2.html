Origami In Engineering - SEI Internship

Overview

During this summer internship at The University of Manchester, I had the opportunity to work alongside highly accomplished engineers in order to build and fly the world’s first foamboard jet aircraft. One of my independent tasks was to laser cut the foamboard and build the prototype, essentially transferring a conceptual CAD model from a computer to a full scale prototype in the lab. This responsibility taught me the importance of patience and attention to detail in engineering, as well as strengthening my laser cutting skills.

Participating in CASCADE week was another incredible experience that I believe will prove to be valuable in my future career as an engineer. Flying the jet at Snowdonia airfield challenged me to apply the aerospace theory I already knew to a practical setting. The collaborative aspect of this project highlighted the value of teamwork and showed me that diverse perspectives can lead to innovative solutions.

The remainder of my internship was spent creating an instruction guide for students to be able to set up autopilot on model foam Riot RC planes. This involved soldering electronics and creating a scaled CAD model of the final aircraft, both skills in which I am accomplished.

Throughout this task, I stepped out of my comfort zone and became proficient in the use of Mission Planner and Radiomaster transmitters. This technical experience allowed me to better understand the intricacies of avionics systems and how they are linked. My autonomy with this project was something I enjoyed, leading me to creating an instruction guide detailing everything I had learned throughout setting up autopilot, with the aim to make building these model aircraft a sustainable option for future 2nd year Aerospace Engineering students.

The highlight of this internship was manufacturing the Riot completely from scratch and then getting the chance to fly it in manual. The excitement and thrill of creating, designing, manufacturing and flying has amplified my enthusiasm for the engineering industry and has provided me with a strong foundation for a future career in this sector.

Feel free to reach out to me if you are curious to see the full 3D model I made to scale of the Riot in Solidworks.
