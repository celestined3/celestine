Aerofoil CFD Analysis

Overview

My goal for this project was to improve my Ansys Fluent skills by investigating the types of flow around an aerofoil for different convection schemes. This allowed me to put into practice my theoretical knowledge about the effects of different grid densities and discretisation schemes on solution accuracy for converged lift and drag coefficient values. I was able to compare my simulations' findings with real life experimental data obtained from a wind tunnel, giving me an insight on the validity of Computational Fluid Dynamics.
