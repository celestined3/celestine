Aircraft Configuration Design

Overview

For this project, I chose three flight trajectories for three different commercial aircraft and needed to use MATLAB to analyse their performance. Producing payload range, SAR and flight envelope charts based on both given and calculated aircraft inputs was challenging, yet rewarding because they were based on real life examples taken directly from Flightradar24. My knowledge of the nomenclature related to this field greatly improved during the completion of this project.
