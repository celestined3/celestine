Sentinel-6B Design Analysis

Overview

For this project, I aimed to apply my knowledge of the existing Sentinel-6A mission to a hypothetical problem (Sentinel-6B), where I analysed the propulsion sensitivity, thermal design, communications link budget, ADCS and power and mass budgets of a satellite in order to enhance my understanding of the various systems on board a spacecraft. This was done using a MATLAB live script, which allowed me to get comfortable with the software very early on at university.

I felt equipped with the necessary knowledge to tackle this project because I had prior experience with GMAT (General Mission Analysis Tool) which taught me absolutely everything I needed to know about orbits.
