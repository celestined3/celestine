Beam Geometry Analysis

Overview

This was a great project for getting comfortable with Abaqus, where I investigated the effects of changing force, length and geometry of a beam on its displacement and deformation. Through Finite Element Analysis, I was able to make conclusions on the effects of changing these parameters for different types of beams and really felt like I got to grips with the software by the end of the project.
