Aircraft Configuration Design

Overview

I was given two flight paths for a commercial aircraft to complete every week: one from Manchester to Tahiti and another from California to Florida. My task here was to design an aircraft completely from scratch that could achieve this, detailing the flight plan, physical design of the aircraft, its weight and much more. I used my SOLIDWORKS skills to design it in 3D, which made visualising the aircraft cross-sections easier and my MATLAB skills to estimate the payload range, constraints on weight and aircraft velocities.

Completing this project really made me appreciate how complicated it is to design a plane because most of the parameters are linked. As with every engineering project, this was not a linear process and it required a lot of iteration before the maths made sense!
