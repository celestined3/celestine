Overview

In one of my first group projects at university, I worked with a team of engineers to design the wing of a small model aircraft. One of the most important parts was the spar, so we tested different beam cross-sections through bending and torsion. For the general sizing of all parts, an Excel spreadsheet was used, which allowed us to easily see how the dimensions were linked and dependent on each other. This was a multi-faceted project because I gained design skills, as well as video filming and editing skills.
