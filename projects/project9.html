Water Bottle Rocket

Overview

The goal for this project was to launch a model rocket and land safely without damage to the payload. Some requirements for a successful mission included: the rocket had to be made out of a plastic soda bottle, the payload was an egg and there had to be a deployable parachute to ensure landing in a designated area. My team worked very well together, combining video editing, Excel, Unity and design skills which resulted in a successful mission. The part of the project I found most intriguing was the fact that this rocket was launched purely through high water pressure - a type of propellant I did not know could work!
