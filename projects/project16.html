Axial Flow Compressor Analysis

Overview

The aim of this project was to investigate the off-design performance of a three-stage axial compressor, analysing its behaviour across a range of rotational speeds and flow conditions. My subsystem requirement was to plot a graph of pressure ratio against mass flow rate for different rotational speeds in order to map the efficiencies of the compressor. This was done by carrying out experiments in the compressor laboratory and using that data in MATLAB code.
