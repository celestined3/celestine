Aerofoil Wind Tunnel Analysis

Overview

I carried out experiments on a NACA0012 aerofoil in a Plint Blower wind tunnel in order to investigate its aerodynamic parameters. The data from these experiments was then processed in Excel and the results are highlighted in the report below. I found it interesting to think critically about why aerodynamic properties like coefficients of lift and drag of this aerofoil changed with angle of attack. Operating the wind tunnel was an incredible experience and using it for a real experiment taught me a lot about its inner workings.
