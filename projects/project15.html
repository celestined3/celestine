Skills Used

Overview

This project included my introduction to Simulink, PID control theory and the Quanser Aero 2 lab equipment. I achieved proficiency in analysing and applying PID controllers through building the original block diagram in MATLAB Simulink myself and doing research on the components of PID control. This project revealed the importance of PID controllers in the real world and gave me confidence in tuning them effectively.
